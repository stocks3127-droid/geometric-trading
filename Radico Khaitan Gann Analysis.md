The rulebook requires monthly/yearly, weekly and daily trends to agree before taking a position.

- **Weekly / intermediate:** Neutral-to-corrective after rejection from the 52-week high. A weekly close below the key support band would be a material deterioration, but is not established by the data snapshot alone.

## 3. Conditional trading plan

This is a mechanical watchlist plan, not a personal recommendation.

### Scenario A — Pullback long

**Condition:** Price trades into ₹4,171–₹4,185, holds the band, then the daily 1-day and 3-day swings turn up. Ideally the reversal is accompanied by expanding volume.

- Confirmation: daily close above the prior three-day high after support holds.
- First reference: ₹4,459–₹4,466.
- Next reference: ₹4,603.
- Major reference: ₹4,747.
- Invalidation: a decisive close below the confirmed swing low; a close below the ₹4,171–₹4,185 cluster would make the setup weaker, not automatically short.

### Scenario B — Breakout continuation

**Condition:** Daily close above ₹4,747, with volume expansion and no immediate failure back below the breakout level.

- First geometric extension: approximately ₹4,891, using the 144-point overlay.
- A failed breakout—close back below the breakout level—invalidates the continuation setup under Rules 200 and 203.
- Do not buy an intraday spike alone; require close and follow-through.

### Scenario C — Bearish correction

**Condition:** Price closes below ₹4,171, forms a lower high on the retest and volume expands on the decline.

- First downside zone: ₹3,883–₹3,889.
- Next reference: approximately ₹3,739.
- A break of ₹3,883–₹3,889 would be a more serious damage signal to the primary bull structure and should be assessed on weekly data.

### What not to do

- Do not chase in the ₹4,237–₹4,459 middle zone without a trigger.
- Do not short solely because P/E is high; valuation and technical timing are different decisions.
- Do not call the 52-week high a confirmed Wave 5 top without wave and volume evidence.
- Do not use an exact stop without the actual swing low and the investor's position size.

### Position sizing from Rule 04

For a cash equity trade, the repository's stock rule states:

```text
Maximum risk budget = 10% × (50% of total stock value above initial margin)
Shares = maximum risk budget ÷ (entry price − stop price)
```

The formula should be adapted to the investor's actual broker, cash account, taxes and slippage. A stop is mandatory immediately after entry under Rule 235; Rule 236's three-day time stop applies to a short-term trading campaign, not automatically to a long-term investment thesis.

## 4. Fundamental risks to monitor

1. **Valuation compression:** At roughly 83x reported earnings, even good results may not prevent a drawdown if growth or margins merely meet expectations.
2. **Premiumisation concentration:** Premium and luxury growth is the central earnings driver; a slowdown in Magic Moments, After Dark, Royal Ranthambore, Rampur or Jaisalmer would weaken the thesis.
3. **State-policy exposure:** Alcohol taxes, distribution rules and policy changes in states such as Maharashtra, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh can change volume and mix quickly.
4. **Input costs and packaging:** Gross-margin expansion partly benefits from favourable input conditions; raw-material and packaging inflation can reverse operating leverage.
5. **Working capital:** Inventory and receivable days remain important in a spirits business; strong reported profit should convert into operating cash flow.
6. **Debt definitions / execution:** Verify the latest consolidated balance sheet and whether the net-debt-free target is achieved on schedule.
7. **Event risk:** The company has indicated a trading-window closure around the next quarterly results; avoid placing a position solely for an unconfirmed earnings-date guess.
